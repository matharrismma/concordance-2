# 07 — Channel and Social

The audience lives with SIXFOLD all week, not just on show night. The YouTube channel is the home
base, and the social accounts are the world talking to itself.

## The kayfabe rulebook

### Stay in
1. **Accounts never break character** in posts, replies or DMs, *except* under the safety rules
   below.
2. **The world is consistent.** Every post has to fit the canon in this bible. If it contradicts
   the canon, either the bible changes first or the post doesn't go out.
3. **One voice per character.** Each account has a named writer and a voice sheet (vocabulary,
   emoji use, posting times).
4. **Story beats go through the writers' room.** Turns, injuries and title changes are never posted
   ahead of the show.

### Never
1. **Never punch down.** Heels insult other characters, not real fans, and never about appearance,
   identity, disability or real-life hardship.
2. **Never use real tragedy.** Glass Night is fictional and must never echo a real disaster's name,
   date, imagery or death toll in a way that could be mistaken for it, or that could hurt someone.
3. **Never impersonate a real organisation.** The Accord's posts must be obviously fictional: a
   fictional crest, and a bio that names it as part of the SIXFOLD series. No real government seals,
   real agencies or real officials.
4. **Never fake a real emergency.** No fake injury posts that look like real medical news, no fake
   arrests, and no hoaxes that could pull in real emergency services.
5. **No real-person kayfabe without consent.** Real celebrities, journalists or brands appear in
   the story only with their agreement.

### Break for safety, always
Someone on the social team is on call during every live show and for 24 hours afterwards.

- **A fan in real distress** (self-harm, threats, crisis): the account drops character, replies
  privately and kindly with crisis resources for that person's region, and escalates to the safety
  lead. No exceptions.
- **A real injury:** if a performer is actually hurt, the kayfabe stops. @sixfold posts a plain,
  human update.
- **Harassment of performers:** fans who go after a heel's performer in real life are blocked and
  reported. The performer gets support. The character never encourages it.
- **Minors:** don't engage with known minors in DMs. Live chat filters and moderation follow
  platform rules for the audience.
- **Real confusion:** if a story beat is being reported as real news, @sixfold clarifies it plainly.
