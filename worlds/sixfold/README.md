# SIXFOLD — Pro Wrestling in the Hex

*A worked-kayfabe pro wrestling promotion set in a world where superheroes are real, banned, and
broke, and the only legal place left to fight is a canvas hexagon where powers stop working.*

> **The pitch in one breath:** Capes can't fight in the streets anymore. So they fight in **the Hex**,
> a sunken six-sided pit where a dampening field called **the Hush** turns gods into athletes.
> Every second is livestreamed. Leave the pit and you **go loud**: your powers come back, the field
> breaks, and you're disqualified. The Hex doesn't care how you left.

**Working title:** SIXFOLD (alternates: *HEXFALL*, *THE HUSH*). It's a placeholder. Run trademark
and channel-name searches before launch.

---

## How the world is layered

SIXFOLD runs on three layers of kayfabe at once. Every piece of content belongs to one of them, and
the layers never contradict each other.

| Layer | What it is | Where the audience meets it |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The World** | Capes, the Aegis Accord, Glass Night, the Hush. The fighting-game universe. | Lore drops, "leaked" footage, character backstories, story arcs |
| **2. The Stream** | SIXFOLD is a pirate livestream that got too big to shut down. The crew, the chat and the host are all *in* the fiction. | The weekly live show, ride-along cams, chat, the post-show stream |
| **3. The Channel** | The real YouTube channel and social accounts, run entirely in character. | Match cuts, Shorts, character accounts, the Accord's "official" press releases |

## The five laws of SIXFOLD

1. **Powers are never shown live.** The stream lives inside the Hush. Powers exist only in leaked
   footage, aftermath and rumour. The live show is pure wrestling, and that's the point.
2. **Capes come off at the rim.** Every hero hangs their cape on a post before the bell. It's the
   ritual of the Hex. The person underneath is what fights.
3. **The Hex doesn't care how you left.** Touch the floor outside the pit and you lose. Thrown,
   dived or fell, it makes no difference.
4. **Everyone with a camera is a character.** Camera ops, the host, the chat moderators and the
   Wardens all exist in the world.
5. **Never break, never punch down.** The accounts stay in character at all times, but a real
   person in real trouble always comes first (see `07`).
