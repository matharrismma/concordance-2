# 04 — Factions and Titles

## Factions

| Faction | Members | What they want | Alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Unregistered** | SOLSTICE, KITE, NIGHT SHIFT, BRIAR (loosely) | To prove grounded heroes still matter. To be heroes again. | Faces |
| **The Aegis Accord** | SENTINEL ZERO, HALCYON, plus unnamed "Accord Observers" in suits at ringside | To discredit SIXFOLD, shut it down and find out what the Hush is | Heels |
| **The Formerly** | MAYDAY, UNDERSTUDY, plus a rotating cast of ex-sidekicks | Revenge on the heroes who used and discarded them | Heels |
| **Independents** | MONOLITH MARA, RAMPART, ÉXODO, VOLTAIC, GLITCH, THE TIDEBREAKERS, DEADLINE | Each their own story | Mixed |
| **The House** | VALE, the Wardens, the Keeper of Posts, the crew | To keep the show on air at any cost | ? |

**Faction rules:** factions are visible on screen. Each one has a colour-coded overlay in the
stream graphics, and the Accord's overlay looks like a government watermark. Factions can
interfere only from inside the Hush (the Ledge, the rim) or by causing chaos on the floor. Any
power use is a DQ for the member it benefits.

## Championships

### The Hex Crown (world championship)
- A six-sided crown-plate belt. Each side carries an engraving of one past champion's cape.
- It's defended at every HEXFALL. The first champion is crowned at the end of the first Sixfold
  tournament.
- **Tradition:** the champion's cape hangs on **Post 1, the Crown Post**, for every match on the
  card, champion's match or not. Taking the Crown means taking the post.

### The Edge Championship (secondary)
- Always defended under **Brink** rules: exits only.
- It belongs to survivors and throwers, the wrestlers who live on the rim.

### The Ledge Titles (tag team)
- Twin belts with a stylised alcove plate.
- **Inaugural champions:** decided in Season One between the TIDEBREAKERS and the Formerly.

### The First Light Trophy
- A one-off honour, presented to GRANDSTAND at his retirement and passed on once a year to the
  wrestler the locker room votes "most heroic." It's kayfabe-honoured, and real-world, it's given
  to whoever the crew and talent think earned it.

## THE SIXFOLD (the annual tournament)

SIXFOLD's equivalent of the fighting-game world tournament: a season-long, six-city event that
crowns or confirms the Hex Crown.

| Stage | Name | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | *Last Light* | A thirty-person battle royal. Exits only. The final eight qualify. |
| **2** | *The Six Cities* | Eight qualifiers travel across six pop-up Hex events (Lagos, Seoul, Mexico City, Osaka, London, Johannesburg). Round-robin, with points for wins and bonus points for exits. |
| **3** | *The Rim* | The top four meet in semi-finals under Brink rules. |
| **4** | *The Crown* | The final at HEXFALL. Standard Hex rules, and the winner takes the Crown Post. |

**The touring Hex:** a modular version of the pit that travels to each city. In the fiction,
SIXFOLD has found Hush "echoes" in other cities, which creates a mystery: where do they come from?
In reality, it's a road set.

**Kayfabe hook:** VALE uses the tour to look for more Hush sites. The Accord follows the tour and
tries to shut down each event before it starts. Every city episode opens with a "will it go ahead?"
cold open.
