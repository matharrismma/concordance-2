# 03 — The Roster

Sixteen launch characters, plus the host and the hidden boss. Each one is built like a
fighting-game select-screen entry: one nation, one silhouette, one power they *can't use*, and one
wrestling style that shows who they are underneath it.

**Design rules for every character**
- **Readable from one still frame.** A distinct colour block, silhouette and cape.
- **Power and style are in tension.** What a cape lost in the Hush should shape how they wrestle.
- **Every cape is original.** No homages to existing comics or games characters. When a design
  starts to resemble one, change it.
- **Wrestle-practical gear.** The cape and big pieces come off at the rim, and what's underneath has
  to be safe to work in.

Alignment key: **Face** (hero), **Heel** (villain), **Tweener** (in between).

## The Unregistered (rebel faces)

### SOLSTICE — *The Last Sunrise*
**Face · Lagos, Nigeria · Heavyweight**
- **Power (outside):** solar absorption and light projection. She was the Accord's number-one
  ranked hero for six straight years.
- **In the Hex:** a strong-style powerhouse with real athleticism. Stiff strikes, deadlifts,
  springboard shoulder tackles. She wrestles like someone who has never needed to and is learning
  out loud.
- **Look:** burnt gold and white, a radiating sunburst across the chest, and a floor-length gold
  cape.
- **Signature:** *Zenith*, a standing shooting-star press. **Finisher:** *High Noon*, a sitout
  powerbomb from the shoulders.
- **Story:** she resigned her Accord badge on live TV the week after Glass Night, saying she
  "couldn't wear it with a straight back." She's been grounded since. She comes to SIXFOLD to prove
  she's a hero without the sun.
- **Rival:** SENTINEL ZERO, her former partner.
- **Handle:** `@solstice.rises`

### KITE — *Ride the Updraft*
**Face · Seoul, South Korea · Junior**
- **Power:** wind riding and short-range flight.
- **In the Hex:** the pit's best high-flyer. She runs the walls and dives off the rim, and lives on
  the teeter. Her matches are built on nearly losing.
- **Look:** sky blue and white, a kite-frame motif across her back, wrist ribbons, and a short
  split cape.
- **Signature:** *Crosswind*, a rim-to-canvas tornado DDT. **Finisher:** *Updraft*, a springboard
  shooting star launched off the rim.
- **Story:** a streamer before she was a hero, and still the biggest IRL streamer on the roster.
  Her personal channel is the most-watched angle in the company. Some people think she joined
  SIXFOLD for content, and she's starting to wonder whether they're right.
- **Rival:** MAYDAY.
- **Handle:** `@kite.live`

### NIGHT SHIFT — *Clocked In*
**Face · Detroit, USA · Heavyweight**
- **Power:** pain transference. He can take other people's injuries into his own body.
- **In the Hex:** a brawler. He absorbs everything, sells nothing until it counts, and is famous for
  comebacks. The joke is that he's a healer who loves getting hit.
- **Look:** EMT navy with reflective hi-vis strips, taped fists, and a cape cut from an old ambulance
  blanket.
- **Signature:** *Triage*, a running crossbody into the wall. **Finisher:** *Code Blue*, a
  chokeslam to a sitout facebuster.
- **Story:** still works real night shifts as an EMT, and gets to the Foundry straight from them. He
  was a first responder at Glass Night, and he knows something about the missing ninety seconds.
- **Rival:** DEADLINE.
- **Handle:** `@nightshift.det`

### BRIAR — *The Thorn That Holds*
**Face · Dublin, Ireland · Light heavyweight**
- **Power:** plant growth, thorns and vines.
- **In the Hex:** a submission specialist. Without vines she uses her limbs, all leglocks and
  wristlocks and things that look like they shouldn't bend that way.
- **Look:** deep green and rose, a thorn-pattern bodysuit, and a cape of woven living ivy that has
  to be watered backstage, which is a running gag.
- **Signature:** *Bramble*, a rolling kneebar. **Finisher:** *Hedgerow*, a modified Gory special
  submission.
- **Story:** the company underdog. She was a small-town hero who never got ranked. Nobody expects her
  to win, and she taps out people who used to sign her posters.
- **Rival:** HALCYON.
- **Handle:** `@briar.holds`

## The Aegis Accord (establishment heels)

### SENTINEL ZERO — *Licensed to Win*
**Heel · Washington DC, USA · Heavyweight**
- **Power:** adaptive armour. His skin hardens to match whatever hits him.
- **In the Hex:** a corporate technician. Efficient, cold and perfect. He has never been hit hard,
  and the Hush is the first time he's ever felt pain.
- **Look:** Accord white and navy, chrome trim, the Aegis crest, and a regulation-length cape with a
  licence number embroidered in the corner.
- **Signature:** *Compliance*, a cobra clutch suplex. **Finisher:** *Executive Order*, a pumphandle
  driver.
- **Story:** the Accord's current number-one hero, sent into SIXFOLD "to demonstrate that it is
  unsafe," which really means destroying it from inside. He and Solstice were partners for nine
  years.
- **Rival:** SOLSTICE.
- **Handle:** `@sentinel.zero.official`, verified, run by an in-world Accord PR team.

### HALCYON — *Calm Before*
**Heel · Reykjavík, Iceland · Light heavyweight**
- **Power:** cryokinesis.
- **In the Hex:** an aristocratic technical wrestler. Bridges, suplexes and chain wrestling. He
  never raises his voice.
- **Look:** glacier white and pale blue, a high collar, and a crystalline cape that hangs stiff on
  the post, as if frozen.
- **Signature:** *Thaw*, a slow-motion bridging armbar. **Finisher:** *Black Ice*, a bridging German
  suplex.
- **Story:** old Accord money, and a third-generation registered hero. He thinks Unregistered capes
  are vermin and the Hush is a gift: "finally, a level floor to prove breeding matters."
- **Rival:** BRIAR.
- **Handle:** `@halcyon`

## The Formerly (ex-sidekick heels)

### MAYDAY — *Distress Signal*
**Heel · London, UK · Junior**
- **Power:** sonic scream.
- **In the Hex:** a fast, mean, cheating junior. Eye rakes, rim baits, and push-outs while the Warden
  is distracted. He's the best in the company at baiting an exit.
- **Look:** distress-flare orange and black, a broken mic-grille mask, and a tattered cape that
  started life as his old sidekick cape, cut down.
- **Signature:** *SOS*, three rapid superkicks. **Finisher:** *Mayday*, a Michinoku driver from the
  rim.
- **Story:** he was the kid sidekick of a famous hero who died at Glass Night. The Accord dropped
  every sidekick from the registry the next morning. He's bitter, brilliant and right about a lot
  of things.
- **Rival:** KITE.
- **Handle:** `@mayday.mayday.mayday`

### UNDERSTUDY — *Second Billing*
**Heel · Melbourne, Australia · Heavyweight**
- **Power:** mimicry. He copies other capes' powers for a few minutes.
- **In the Hex:** a mimic in a different sense: he steals opponents' moves mid-match. Every match he
  wrestles is a mirror.
- **Look:** a reversible grey costume with a blank mask that he re-paints for every opponent. The
  cape is plain grey.
- **Signature:** whatever his opponent's signature is. **Finisher:** *The Reprise*, his opponent's
  own finisher.
- **Story:** MAYDAY's enforcer. He spent his career as a stand-in, and wants to know what he's like
  when he isn't copying anyone. He hasn't found out yet.
- **Handle:** `@understudy`

## Independents and tweeners

### MONOLITH MARA — *Mountain in Motion*
**Tweener · Tbilisi, Georgia · Super-heavyweight**
- **Power:** stone skin and immense mass.
- **In the Hex:** a monster. Her slams are exits. The Hush makes her mortal, but she's still
  enormous and terrifyingly strong.
- **Look:** slate grey and ochre, carved-rock armour plates, and a heavy felt cape like a shepherd's
  cloak.
- **Signature:** *Rockfall*, a gorilla press thrown over the rim. **Finisher:** *Landslide*, a
  sitout chokebomb.
- **Story:** a disaster-relief cape who carried buildings off people. She's never had a fight she
  could lose, and in the Hex she can. She starts as a monster heel and turns face after losing to
  BRIAR.
- **Rival:** RAMPART.
- **Handle:** `@monolith.mara`

### ÉXODO — *The Mask Walks Through*
**Face · Mexico City, Mexico · Light heavyweight**
- **Power:** intangibility, the ability to phase through matter.
- **In the Hex:** lucha libre, all headscissors, armdrags and dives. Without phasing, he's finding
  out how solid he is.
- **Look:** a black and silver mask with a door motif, a sash, and a translucent mesh cape.
- **Signature:** *Umbral*, a rim-walk headscissors. **Finisher:** *Paso Final*, a rolling
  cutter.
- **Story:** the mask is his real identity. His family has worn it for three generations, both as
  heroes and as luchadores. He's the first to wear it in the Hex. If he ever loses it in a Cape vs.
  Cape match, it's over for all of them.
- **Rival:** UNDERSTUDY, who keeps painting Éxodo's mask on his own face.
- **Handle:** `@exodo.oficial`

### VOLTAIC — *Short Fuse*
**Tweener · Osaka, Japan · Junior**
- **Power:** electrokinesis.
- **In the Hex:** an all-action speed junior with short, explosive matches. He's terrible at pacing.
- **Look:** yellow and black lightning-chevron gear, spiked hair, and a stubby cape with copper wire
  stitching.
- **Signature:** *Overload*, a running knee to the corner post. **Finisher:** *Short Circuit*, a
  spinning DDT.
- **Story:** a hero who blew out a city's power grid by accident and has been grounded ever since.
  He's a hothead who wants to prove he can be controlled, and he's the most likely cape on the roster
  to go loud by accident.
- **Rival:** GLITCH.
- **Handle:** `@voltaic.jp`

### RAMPART — *Unbroken*
**Face · Johannesburg, South Africa · Heavyweight**
- **Power:** force fields.
- **In the Hex:** defensive. He's never been knocked down in his career, and in the Hush everyone
  can knock him down. A wall-to-wall powerhouse learning to be vulnerable.
- **Look:** brick red and gold, a hexagonal shield-plate motif (a nod to the pit), and a short
  armoured cape.
- **Signature:** *Bulwark*, a running shoulder block into the wall. **Finisher:** *Breach*, a
  spinning sitout spinebuster.
- **Story:** a city defender who held a shield over a stadium during a riot for eleven hours. He's
  in SIXFOLD to find out whether he's brave or just bulletproof.
- **Rival:** MONOLITH MARA.
- **Handle:** `@rampart.za`

### GLITCH — *Signal Lost*
**Tweener · Mumbai, India · Junior**
- **Power:** technopathy, digital control.
- **In the Hex:** an unpredictable, funky junior style. Off-rhythm offence and fake-outs.
- **Look:** a black base with a pixel-corruption pattern that animates under UV light, an LED visor,
  and a cape with a printed "NO SIGNAL" test card.
- **Signature:** *Buffering*, a slow-motion fake-out into a superkick. **Finisher:** *Hard Reset*, a
  spinning brainbuster.
- **Story:** the only cape whose power *works through the stream*. Glitch hijacks SIXFOLD's
  broadcast, overlays, chat and other wrestlers' accounts (see `06` and `07`). Is GLITCH breaking
  the rules? The Hush doesn't reach the internet.
- **Rival:** VOLTAIC.
- **Handle:** `@gl1tch` (posts occasionally appear on *other* characters' accounts)

### THE TIDEBREAKERS — RIPTIDE & UNDERTOW
**Face tag team · Cebu, Philippines**
- **Power:** hydrokinesis, as siblings who share it. Each is weaker alone.
- **In the Hex:** fast double-team tag specialists, built around Ledge tags and flawless
  timing.
- **Look:** sea-glass teal and coral, mirrored designs, and capes that clip together into a single
  wave pattern when they're hung on adjacent posts.
- **Team finisher:** *Rogue Wave*, a powerbomb and blockbuster combination.
- **Story:** typhoon rescuers who saved a city, then got grounded when the Accord ruled that their
  shared power "couldn't be licensed to a single individual." The Hex is the first place they've
  been allowed to work as a team in years.
- **Handle:** `@tidebreakers`

### GRANDSTAND — *The First Light*
**Face legend · São Paulo, Brazil · Heavyweight · age 58**
- **Power:** invulnerability to heat, with fire immunity. He was the first verified cape in
  history.
- **In the Hex:** a veteran ring general who wrestles in old-school style: headlocks, crowd work and
  a few big moments per match.
- **Look:** fire-brigade red and brass, the original suit from forty years ago (re-made), and a
  battered cape covered in faded sponsor patches from the Golden Run.
- **Finisher:** *Curtain Call*, a slow lariat. The whole crowd counts it in.
- **Story:** the living history of capes. He's both the mentor and the one who knows too much. He
  was in Rotterdam the day before Glass Night. Retirement match at the first HEXFALL.
- **Handle:** `@grandstand` (posts like a grandfather who just learned the internet)

### DEADLINE — *Time's Up*
**Heel · Toronto, Canada · Heavyweight**
- **Power:** time perception. He experiences a second as ten.
- **In the Hex:** a patient, surgical heel. He counts down opponents out loud, and is a submission
  master. He's furious that the Hush makes him live in real time.
- **Look:** charcoal suit-style gear, a watch-face sigil, and a long tailored cape with a pocket
  watch on a chain.
- **Signature:** *Countdown*, ten shoulder thrusts counted aloud. **Finisher:** *Final Notice*, a
  crossface.
- **Story:** an Accord investigator assigned to Glass Night. He's seen the missing ninety seconds,
  and he says nothing.
- **Rival:** NIGHT SHIFT.
- **Handle:** `@deadline`

## The host and the boss

### VALE — *The Host*
**Promoter · origin unknown · non-wrestler (mostly)**
- The masked host and founder of SIXFOLD LIVE. He wears a featureless chrome half-mask and a
  tailored black coat, and speaks to chat by name.
- He was formerly **LODESTAR**, a top hero presumed dead at Glass Night. It's revealed at the end of
  Season One (see `05`).
- **Role:** like any fighting-game tournament host, he's the organiser with an agenda. He built
  SIXFOLD to draw capes into the Hush. Why?
- He'll wrestle exactly once a year, maybe.

### NULL — *The Quiet*
**Hidden final boss · not yet revealed**
- The Hush is not a place. **It's a person.**
- Under the Foundry, in the original detention cell the Hex was built over, sits the unknown cape
  from Glass Night. Their power is to switch off other powers, and they never asked for it. The
  field leaks upward through the floor of the pit.
- NULL caused the ninety missing seconds. VALE, DEADLINE and GRANDSTAND each know part of the
  truth.
- **First appearance:** the Season One finale. **First match:** Season Two.
- In the Hex, NULL is just a person too, which is why they're the most dangerous wrestler alive:
  they're the only one who has ever fought like this.
