# 05 — Season One: *GOING LOUD*

**Twelve weekly episodes of SIXFOLD LIVE, plus HEXFALL I** (the first premium event).

**The question the season asks:** what are heroes, when nobody has powers?
**The question the season answers:** where the Hush comes from.

Season One stays in a single venue. It runs a compressed **First Sixfold** at the Foundry to crown
the first Hex Crown. The six-city tour begins in Season Two, once there's an audience to tour to.

---

## The threads

| Thread | Arc |
|---|---|
| **A: The Crown** | SOLSTICE vs. SENTINEL ZERO. Ex-partners, the Unregistered against the Accord. Ends at HEXFALL. |
| **B: The Broadcast** | The Accord tries to take SIXFOLD off the air, and GLITCH keeps it on (or hijacks it). |
| **C: The Sidekicks** | MAYDAY and the Formerly against KITE, and the question of who gets to be a hero. |
| **D: Glass Night** | NIGHT SHIFT, DEADLINE, GRANDSTAND and VALE each hold a piece. It builds to the reveal. |
| **E: The Monster** | MONOLITH MARA destroys everyone, until BRIAR makes her ask for quiet. Mara turns. |

---

## Episodes

### EP 1: *Four Hundred Viewers*
- **Cold open:** a vertical phone stream, shaky, of a fight in a concrete room. It cuts to black and
  shows a view counter: **400**.
- VALE opens the first official SIXFOLD LIVE, explains the Hush and the rules, and introduces the
  Wardens.
- The first cape ritual on screen: SOLSTICE hangs her gold cape on Post 4. Chat floods.
- **Main event:** SOLSTICE vs. VOLTAIC. A fast, raw opener. VOLTAIC nearly goes loud when he
  touches the rim, and the Warden's tablet spikes. SOLSTICE wins with *High Noon*.
- **Closing beat:** a press release scrolls across the stream overlay. The Aegis Accord "condemns
  this broadcast." The feed cuts.

### EP 2: *Badge*
- SENTINEL ZERO arrives with Accord Observers and a legal injunction. VALE tears it up on camera.
- SENTINEL ZERO announces that he'll "compete under protest" to prove SIXFOLD is unsafe.
- **Debut:** MONOLITH MARA throws RAMPART over the rim in under three minutes. It's the first exit
  DQ on the show.
- KITE's ride-along stream catches MAYDAY in the Lobby: "Nice cape. Shame about the career."

### EP 3: *Sidekick*
- MAYDAY's in-ring promo: Glass Night, the registry purge, every sidekick thrown away. The Formerly
  debut, with UNDERSTUDY as the enforcer.
- **Match:** KITE vs. MAYDAY. MAYDAY wins by baiting KITE into a dive that overshoots the rim.
- First **teeter** of the series: ÉXODO hangs off the outside wall for twelve seconds in his match
  against UNDERSTUDY before climbing back to win.

### EP 4: *Signal Lost*
- Mid-show, the stream is **hijacked**. The overlays corrupt, chat fills with pixel noise, and an
  unknown voice says: "They're trying to shut you off. I won't let them."
- **GLITCH** is revealed in the Rim Bar and declares SIXFOLD "protected."
- The Accord responds with a real-time "takedown attempt." The feed drops to a backup low-res camera
  for six minutes and the show keeps going, which is played completely straight.
- **Match:** NIGHT SHIFT vs. DEADLINE. DEADLINE counts down every move aloud. NIGHT SHIFT wins with
  a comeback, but DEADLINE whispers something to him afterwards and NIGHT SHIFT goes white.

### EP 5: *Last Light*
- **The First Sixfold begins:** a sixteen-person *Last Light* battle royal, exits only.
- Signature moments: KITE survives three teeters. GRANDSTAND gets the crowd to count *Curtain
  Call* and eliminates HALCYON. MONOLITH MARA throws out five people.
- **The final eight:** SOLSTICE, SENTINEL ZERO, KITE, MAYDAY, NIGHT SHIFT, MONOLITH MARA, BRIAR
  and GRANDSTAND.
- BRIAR is the last qualifier, after she hangs on the rim through a full Mara charge.

### EP 6: *Mortal*
- **Quarter-finals, part 1:** SENTINEL ZERO vs. GRANDSTAND. The legend bleeds. SENTINEL wins with
  a disrespectful pin: one foot on the old man's chest.
- **Quarter-finals, part 2:** KITE vs. MAYDAY II. KITE wins with *Updraft*, landing inches inside
  the rim. A rim judge calls for replay, and the replay itself becomes a viral Short.
- GRANDSTAND's post-match Locker stream: "I was in Rotterdam the day before. I've never told anyone
  why."

### EP 7: *Quiet*
- **Quarter-finals, part 3:** MONOLITH MARA vs. BRIAR. Mara has never lost. BRIAR survives
  everything and locks in *Hedgerow*. **Mara asks for quiet**, and the Foundry goes silent.
- Mara's turn: she hangs her cape on BRIAR's post in respect.
- **Quarter-finals, part 4:** SOLSTICE vs. NIGHT SHIFT. A face vs. face match that's mutual and
  respectful. SOLSTICE wins.

### EP 8: *The Cape That Wouldn't Come Off*
- SENTINEL ZERO refuses the cape ritual before his semi-final against KITE: "An Accord cape is
  government property." **He forfeits.** KITE advances without a match.
- **Twist:** it was deliberate. The Accord wanted footage of SIXFOLD "forcing" a hero to disrobe.
  Within an hour there's an Accord press campaign: *SIXFOLD STRIPS HEROES OF IDENTITY.*
- VALE's response: "Then come back without it."

### EP 9: *Reinstatement*
- **Semi-final:** SOLSTICE vs. BRIAR, under Brink rules. SOLSTICE wins with an exit.
- The Accord files an appeal, and VALE shocks everyone by **reinstating SENTINEL ZERO** into a
  match against KITE for the final spot, on the condition that his cape comes off.
- SENTINEL ZERO accepts and takes off his cape, *revealing that his licence number has been
  stitched onto his skin-tight gear underneath.*
- **SENTINEL ZERO vs. KITE:** a heel masterclass. He baits her to the rim and pushes her off it.
  KITE is exited, and SENTINEL ZERO advances.
- MAYDAY, watching from the Ledge, *applauds*.

### EP 10: *Ninety Seconds*
- The Glass Night episode. DEADLINE gives NIGHT SHIFT a sealed Accord evidence bag on camera.
- NIGHT SHIFT watches it in the Locker stream (we see his face only, never the footage).
- The Ledge Title final, TIDEBREAKERS vs. the Formerly: the TIDEBREAKERS win with *Rogue Wave*.
  They're the first SIXFOLD tag champions.
- **Closing beat:** NIGHT SHIFT confronts VALE in the Lobby. "You were there. You were there and
  you're supposed to be dead."

### EP 11: *Lodestar*
- VALE takes off the mask, live. He's **LODESTAR**, the hero presumed dead at Glass Night.
- His confession: he found the Hush in the ruins of Glass Night, followed it to the detention cell
  under the Foundry, and built SIXFOLD on top of it, "so nobody would ever ask what's underneath."
- GRANDSTAND fills in a piece: Lodestar asked for his help the day before Glass Night, to "bring
  someone in quietly."
- **Final hype:** SOLSTICE and SENTINEL ZERO face to face on the rim, with the Crown Post between
  them.

### EP 12: *Rim*
- The go-home show.
- **GRANDSTAND's farewell:** a retirement ceremony, the First Light Trophy, and his cape retired to
  the Foundry's rafters.
- GLITCH warns the audience that the Accord is planning to "flip the switch" at HEXFALL.
- **Main event:** a Six-Post with the full roster, which collapses into a brawl and ends with the
  lights flickering. **The Warden's tablet reads zero.** For a heartbeat, the Hush is *off*. Then
  it's back. Fade out.

## HEXFALL I: *The Crown*

| Match | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ÉXODO vs. UNDERSTUDY (Cape vs. Cape) | ÉXODO wins | UNDERSTUDY loses his plain grey cape, and with it his only identity. He's unmasked as a nobody, and the crowd *cheers him*. It sets up his Season Two face run. |
| MONOLITH MARA & BRIAR vs. HALCYON & DEADLINE | MARA & BRIAR win | Mara's first match as a face. |
| KITE vs. MAYDAY III (Brink) | KITE wins | KITE exits MAYDAY with a rim *Crosswind* and finally wins the feud. MAYDAY's Locker stream afterwards is the most emotional moment of the season. |
| TIDEBREAKERS vs. VOLTAIC & GLITCH (Ledge Titles) | TIDEBREAKERS retain | Mid-match, GLITCH hijacks the replay screen to show Accord vans outside the Foundry. |
| **SOLSTICE vs. SENTINEL ZERO (the Hex Crown)** | **SOLSTICE wins** | See below. |

### The main event
Twenty-five minutes of the best match the company has. Late in the match, the Accord cuts the
Hush.

The lights flicker and the tablet flatlines. Both wrestlers feel their powers come back *while
standing inside the pit*. SENTINEL ZERO's armour starts to form. He could win right now.

**He doesn't.** He looks at SOLSTICE, lets his armour fall away, and steps toward her.

The Hush comes back on its own, from *below*. SOLSTICE hits *High Noon*. One, two, three.
**SOLSTICE is the first Hex Crown.** She hangs her gold cape on the Crown Post.

### The final scene
The stream doesn't end. A **Shadow camera** (an unassigned one, with no wrestler) walks down the
service stairs under the pit, into the original detention cell.

A figure sits on the floor in the dark, very still. Grey clothes, bare feet. No cape.

They look up into the camera, and the stream audio goes completely silent, with no music and no
room tone.

On-screen text, typed by no one: **`NULL`**

*End of Season One.*

## Season Two seeds
- NULL steps into the Hex.
- SENTINEL ZERO is fired by the Accord on air, and becomes an Unregistered tweener.
- The six-city tour, with Hush echoes in other cities. Are there more NULLs?
- UNDERSTUDY, without an identity, as the company's new everyman babyface.
- The Accord's answer: their own televised "sanctioned" hero league, built to compete with SIXFOLD.
