The One Body

The calculation map and the theory FLOOR, drawn as one figure. The inner disk is every calculation, placed in the sector of its canonical form — sharing a sector is sharing a computation (same_form). Each calculation then throws a chord outward to the theory it stands on (rests_on); those theories are the lit points of the FLOOR on the rim, sized by how many calculations lean on them.
