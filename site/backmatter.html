Study Tables

The reference tables a good study Bible keeps in the back — weights and measures, the names of God, the parables and miracles, an introduction to each book, a topical index. Every entry points into Scripture; where careful scholarship genuinely disagrees — how long a cubit is, when a book was written — both positions are carried, never one flattened verdict. Names of God carry their Strong's numbers and open into the real lexicon.
