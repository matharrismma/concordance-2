The Playbook

The Body's testimony of faithful obedience — what was done in faith, what came of it, and what was corrected.

Every entry begins with a confession ("I may be wrong…"), stands under Scripture, and is confirmed only when two brothers affirm it and a wait has passed. It is not Scripture and binds no conscience — testimony, so wisdom compounds without becoming doctrine.

Opening the record…

Confirmed testimony is affirmed by the Body — it is never the library's verified claim, and never Scripture. Failure is not hidden here; a testimony that bore no fruit is pruned (John 15:2), and the trail of a person learning stays whole.

To add your own testimony you sign it with your key on your own device — the key never travels. Read the Word on the Scripture surface, or start at the desk.

We are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses. — Hebrews 12:1
