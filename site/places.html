Atlas — the places of the Bible

Real coordinates, honestly held. A solid mark is a secure identification. A hollow mark is a genuinely disputed site — tap it and the candidates are named, none silently chosen. And some places (Eden, Emmaus, Tarshish, Ophir) are listed with no mark at all, because an honest blank beats a confident guess. Every coordinate is cross-checked against an independent gazetteer in this project's test suite.
