The Spiral

The calculations laid on a logarithmic spiral — the scale-free, self-similar curve, the same golden-angle geometry the theory map already grows on. Every bead is a calculation; the coil is ordered so each domain is one continuous arc of color. Woven on top is the connective tissue: for each canonical form, a thread through all its beads — and since a form recurs across domains, its thread reaches between the arcs. The gold weave IS the set of domain connections; the brightest threads are the forms that bridge the most fields.
