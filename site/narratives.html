Storyboards — the common narratives

Scripture tells certain stories again and again — exile and return, the barren woman bears, down to the pit and raised to life. Each storyboard here is charted from the Bible's own tellings: real people, verified references. The movements are one shared vocabulary, so you can lift a single component — testing, reversal, return — and trace it through every story that carries it.

You may recognise yourself in many of these storyboards at different times of your life. That recognition is the point — and its limit. These are reference points, not identities: we are not saying you are that person; the characteristics have been displayed, and the same faithful God met them there.
