The fellowship of believers

A network of believers who serve one another — offer, need, collaborate, create. Not a church and not a tithe (the churches are welcome here as nodes); sovereign, with no account and no directory of persons. Your key is your node.

This door opens with confession. The believers around you are not shown at once — the way opens as you walk it. To enter the fellowship, confess with your mouth that Jesus Christ is Lord and Messiah (Romans 10:9). Your key signs it; the confession, not your name, opens the door.
