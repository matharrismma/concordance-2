The Calculation Map

Every calculation placed by its canonical form — the way a Smith chart places every impedance on one disk. A sector is one form; each dot is a calculation, colored by domain. And the model is fractal: a coarse form is a form-of-forms, so the three families that ran too wide — ratio, exponential, modular — are drawn as tinted super-sectors split into their finer children. 343 calculations · 37 leaf forms under 3 split families.
