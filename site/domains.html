Connections Between Domains

Every domain is a node on the ring, grouped into families. Each thread is a master equation, drawn through every domain it connects — the thread is the connection, and its colour is the formula that makes it. Where many threads pass through one node, that field is a hub of reality's computation (its node grows). 38 connected domains, joined by the 25 master equations.
