# The Five Doors — the 15→5 audit

Matt, 2026-10-02: *"We want to be the most useful tool possible. We don't need to be for everyone, but we need to serve excellent in our core areas. We also need to ensure the user experiences one cohesive experience. I don't want 15 things. I want 1-5 amazing things."*

This is the map, not the change: every page, agent tool and route assigned to ONE of five verbs — or to PLUMBING (kept, never advertised), FOLD (merged into a verb's single door), RETIRE (redirect or remove). Each assignment carries its reason. **The assignments are proposals for Matt's approval.**

## The five

- **CHECK** — concordance.html — one box: bring any claim, get the verdict, the worked trail and the seal
- **FIND** — explore.html + search — the keeping (872k PD cards), the Atlas as ONE map with views, one lookup
- **WALK** — situations.html — a situation in, ONE next step out: path · pattern · the figures' words · the anchor that fits
- **KEEP** — profile.html — your deck, your receipts, your node; shared keeping (fellowship) behind it
- **WORD** — bible.html — Scripture at depth for families: passage · cross-refs · original words · commentary · harmony · timeline

## The first three changes (after approval)

1. **One front door, five verbs.** `concordance.html` and `index.html` show the same five doors (Word first on .org, Check first on .com) plus *about*. `com.html` keeps its prose and loses its lobby. `halls.html`, the seven Atlas pages, the Word's five pages, the Workshop twins and the demos fold into their doors as tabs/views. Pages marked RETIRE become redirects (the clean-URL layer already serves both addresses; a redirect is one line in `MOVED_TO_ORG`-style tables).
2. **The `core` MCP profile = the 17 doors.** The other 80 tools stay exactly where they are, in their profiles; `tools/list` on `core` stops listing them. `test_mcp_profiles` pins the partition; the golden moves deliberately.
3. **One cohesive trail.** Every door ends the same way: a verdict or a card, the trail, a seal or a receipt, and *one next step* — the Walk's output format becomes the house format for all five. No door hands a reader a lobby.

## What stays off the five on purpose

Operator and node surfaces (`keep.html`, `steward.html`, `/health*`, `/build.json`, `/capabilities`, `/mcp/*` mounts, the mesh's door/invite/tend, the steward's budget, the training loop's `/wants`/`/unchecked`) are plumbing: they must exist, be documented on `connect.html` and `llms.txt`, and never be a reader's third click.
