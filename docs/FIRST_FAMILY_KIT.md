# The First Family Kit — put it in real hands this week

One goal: **one family, three tasks, one honest conversation about where they stalled.**
Nothing else counts as done. (PIVOT-4, the lever above every other lever.)

## What you hand them

1. **The address**: narrowhighway.com — free, no account, nothing to install.
2. **The microSD** (optional, for the offline-minded): copy the folder
   `D:\NarrowHighway-Releases\field-library\field-library-v1-2026-08-05T164837Z`
   onto any 1GB+ card. Tell them: "813MB — 1,174 books, 1,012 government manuals
   and bulletins, the whole Bible, and a search that works with no internet.
   Run `python verify_image.py` inside it if you want proof nobody altered it."
3. **The one-pager below** — print it or text it.

## The one-pager (give this to the family)

> **Narrow Highway** — a library and study desk, free, no account.
>
> Would you try three things this week and tell me where you got stuck?
> Honest complaints are the most valuable thing you can give me.
>
> 1. **With your child**: go to narrowhighway.com/read.html and do one
>    reading lesson together. Note anything confusing or slow.
> 2. **For your house**: on the front page, ask one practical question you
>    actually care about — "when do I plant potatoes," "how do I treat a
>    bee sting," anything. Was the answer worth reading?
> 3. **For your soul**: open narrowhighway.com/bible.html, look up a verse
>    you know, and tap a word to see the original language.
>
> Then tell me: what stalled you, what surprised you, what would make you
> come back tomorrow? There are no wrong answers — if it confused you, that
> is a fact about the site, not about you.

## What to watch for (your notebook, not theirs)

- Where does the child lose interest in the reading lesson — and at what minute?
- Does the parent type a question or browse? What EXACT words do they type?
- First moment of delight, if any. First moment of friction, always.
- Do they ever find the Bible page without being pointed to it?

## The loop back

Whatever they say, bring it here raw — their words, not a summary. Each
stall becomes the next work item; that is the steering instrument the
traffic logs can never be.
