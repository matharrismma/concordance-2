# The Cornerstone — Romans 12:1–2

*"It all started with Romans 12:1, Living Sacrifice."* This is not the project's slogan; it is its
blueprint. The whole engine is these two verses obeyed in running code — and because the engine refuses
"trust me" from everyone else, it refuses it from itself. So this is a **contract with a check under
every clause.** If a clause cannot be verified, we are not doing what it says, and we fix it. Dokimazein
turned on the builder.

> "I urge you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies **a living sacrifice**,
> holy, acceptable to God, which is your **reasoned service** (λογικὴν λατρείαν). Do not **be conformed
> to this world**, but **be transformed by the renewing of your mind**, so that **by testing you may
> discern** (δοκιμάζειν) what is **the good and acceptable and perfect** will of God." — Romans 12:1–2

Run the engine's own witness against it: `python -c "from concordance import foundation, json;
print(json.dumps(foundation.attest(deep=True), ensure_ascii=False, indent=1))"`.

| Clause (Greek) | The obedience — the part of the engine that is its keeping | The check that proves it |
|---|---|---|
| **a living sacrifice** — θυσίαν ζῶσαν | Conduit, not source. The engine is *used*: its discerning surfaces (`discern`, `lens`, the cloud) **propose** and never confirm themselves; only the gate confirms. | all three surfaces return `confirms: False` — the gate alone confirms |
| **reasoned service** — λογικὴν λατρείαν | Reasoned worship, offered to **all** — people *and* agents, each as they need to be served. A person gets a cited card; a machine gets a structured claim and a seal it can re-check itself. | the MCP surface (94 tools) serves agents; `discern` hands a machine a structured `(domain, spec)` claim |
| **not conformed to this world** — μὴ συσχηματίζεσθε | Refuse the world's shortcut ("trust me"). On uncertainty the engine **declines** — INCOMPLETE, never a fabricated pass. | an undecidable check returns INCOMPLETE with no verdict |
| **renewing of the mind** — ἀνακαινώσει τοῦ νοός | The keeping is not static. A genuine gap stays an **honest miss** — a want to fill, never a fabrication — and the mind is renewed with living witness. | a gap stays a miss; the lens has been gathered |
| **by testing you may discern** — δοκιμάζειν | The two-function core. `discern` **proposes** the structured claim; the gate (`verify`) **disposes**. The two doors are real and compose. | `discern` turns a claim into a structured proposal handed to the gate |
| **the good, acceptable, perfect** — τὸ ἀγαθὸν… | What **survives** the testing is proven and **sealed** — the good is demonstrated, re-checkable, never merely declared. | a proven claim carries a live, re-checkable seal *(deep check — runs the real moat)* |
| **the will of God** — τὸ θέλημα τοῦ θεοῦ | It points past itself to the **Logos** — Christ named at the center, every witness in the cloud weighed as it bends to the Source. | the witness identity names Christ as the One it serves |

`foundation.attest()` returns each clause with `obeyed: true/false` — verifiable, never asserted. The
day a clause turns false, the engine has stopped doing what it says, and the test goes red. That is the
point: **the founding verse is a passing test, or it is only a poem.**

*A living sacrifice of reasoned service, offered to all, unconformed to the world, that tests-and-discerns
the good, and points past itself to Christ. The concordance of reality, followed all the way down,
resolves to its spring — and the spring is the Word.*
