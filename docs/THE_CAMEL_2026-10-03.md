## The rule of known branches

- **Every tier is a named place with a hash.** A tar carries its sha256; the field pack carries MANIFEST.json and a stdlib re-hasher; a seal carries a content hash. A copy that arrives unverified is not a copy.
- **Every source is a named source.** The Library of Congress, the Internet Archive's public-domain window, Project Gutenberg, CODATA, the World English Bible. The tortoise never takes whatever a search engine says.
- **Every peer is a known peer.** The mesh opens to a confession bound to a key; a post is signed; a stranger may listen and ask, never write.
- **Random is admitted only through a gate.** The public-domain license gate, the airlock, the test of the spirits, the alignment tiers, the executable-as-given sweep.
- **A resource offered is taken and never depended on.** A model (BYOM), a partner's radio, a mirror: the core runs with none of them.
